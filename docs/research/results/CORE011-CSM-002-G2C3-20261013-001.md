## Controlled modes and results

Mode definitions were frozen: `DOTNET_TieredCompilation` / `DOTNET_TieredPGO` = `1/1`, `1/0`, `0/0`; `DOTNET_TC_QuickJit=1`, `DOTNET_TC_QuickJitForLoops=0`, `DOTNET_ReadyToRun=1` in all processes. `warmups=0` means the first public call is immediately measured; `warmups=64` means 64 complete validated but untimed public calls precede 32 measured calls.

Values below are **last-16 median wall milliseconds per full public CSM call**. They are descriptive *within each runner*, not confidence bounds. *Verify* = `ChunkManifest.VerifyManifestAsync`; *Reader* = complete, validated 31-entry `ManifestReader` enumeration.

**Direct outcomes:**

1. **Strong runtime-setting association:** `TieredCompilation=0` yields stable median Verify/Reader times within each machine on all four preregistered cold/hot combinations (9V45 ~0.6–0.7ms; 9V74 ~0.9–1.0ms), often substantially below tiered-mode means. **This is a diagnostic configuration comparison only**, not authorization to disable tiering by default for ChunkShift or an attribution specifically to ManifestId.
2. **Within-process transitions on 9V74:** tiered ON / PGO OFF cold baseline starts near 6.9–7.0ms after the initial cold JIT call, falls near **call 22–23** to ~3.1–3.2ms; with **64 prewarm calls** it is ~0.93–1.04ms. Tiered ON / PGO ON cold `Reader` also transitions near call 23–25 (about 7.0 → 3.5ms); `Verify` cold remains ~6.5–6.9ms through call 32, but after 64 prewarms is ~0.875ms. These nontrivial timings are strongly consistent with execution-phase effects, but method-level Tier0/Tier1 events were **not captured**, so the exact JIT mechanism is unproved.
3. **Hardware-specific divergence:** 9V45 stays near 2.6–2.75ms in tiered ON modes even after 64 prewarms; 9V74's PGO-off regime reaches near tiered-OFF performance after that warmup, while PGO-on `Reader` still does not. The two CPUs are unequal; native feature/backend selection, JIT heuristics, frequency and other runtime differences are not independently isolated.
4. **CPU versus wall:** per-call process CPU closely tracks wall in representative steady phases; no cgroup CPU throttling events were reported in either run's retained guard summary. This weakens a pure cgroup-quota explanation. GC/pause raw counters were preserved but do not isolate JIT or SIMD paths by themselves.
5. **No batching experiment was run here.** All 24 consumers used exactly the **same pristine compiled production Core**, and all 768 public operations produced the expected canonical manifest identities and full Reader count/offset oracles. G2-C3 cannot establish speedup of stage64, stage256 or allocation-gated candidate.
