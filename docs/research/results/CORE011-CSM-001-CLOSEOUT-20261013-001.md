## Scope of decision

This is **a final schedule/product-scope disposition of the research wave**, *not* a statistically powered, previously approved product optimization decision. The frozen [G1-J preregistration protocol](../../benchmarks/CORE011-CSM-001-G1J-QUALIFICATION-PROTOCOL.md) and its unchanged [machine contract](../../../benchmarks/experiments/core011-csm-g1j-contract.v1.json) explicitly set:

- `stage=preregistration`;
- `decisionRunsAllowed=false`;
- `budgetsLocked=false`;
- all CPU, wall, peak/idle memory, retention, allocations and confidence bounds **null**;
- required real versioned mixed-reuse corpus, independent experimental blocks, Windows and supported NativeAOT validation not completed for a release-bound reader change.

No evidence from exploratory measurements may be relabelled as an ADOPT decision, and neither an implementation of a hypothetical optimization nor a claim of cross-architecture performance qualification follows from a green lab smoke.

**The distinction matters:** `DEFER` means "insufficient frozen product decision gates; no changes for Core 0.1.1", not "the experiment conclusively found no CPU/LOH win" and not "all original #154 acceptance checkboxes were fulfilled". In contrast, J2I/J2J established a narrower deterministic size **NO_GAIN_ON_FROZEN_PAIRS** for exactly two versioned managed DLL pairs.

## Compatibility / release decision

- `NO_ADOPT`: **no CSM reader, writer, ManifestId, decode cache, pooling strategy, batch-size or CRC implementation from these experiments is committed to production `src/`.**
- Keep the shipped profile `fastcdc.gear.chunkshift.v1.64k` (16/64/256 KiB). **Do not register or alias lab16k** merely due to exact reusable chunks; the physical CSP result on the two original managed pairs is negative.
- Keep CSM v1, CSP v1, `ChunkId`, `ManifestId`, `FileDigest`, `CRC32C`, failure ordering, public API and NuGet dependency surface unchanged by this closeout.
- Research-only implementation PRs, golden/malformed tests, GitHub-hosted workflows and independent byte forensics remain in `main`; they are **not** production adoption.
- Parent [#152](https://github.com/definitely-stable/ChunkShift/issues/152) remains open. This closeout does **NOT** certify the Core 0.1.1 release, NativeAOT/API binary compatibility across all candidates, or the readiness of other parent issues #127/#137/#153. Any later production optimization needs its *own* frozen budget, independent representative workload and compatibility decision, not a reinterpretation of these descriptive samples.

**Final scope decision:** `DEFER` CORE011-CSM-001 through 006 for 0.1.1; close the present research ticket #154 *as explicitly deferred, not completed as an adopted optimization*. Archive the negative FastCDC16k finding. Do not start G1-J2K or a new profile bake-off under this closed wave.
