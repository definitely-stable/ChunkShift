## Interpretation and limitations

- Correctness gate passed for the sampled data and fragmentation patterns; release correctness remains dependent on the independent decoder, frozen official vectors, fuzzing and x64/ARM64 JIT/NativeAOT gates.
- Read calls increase from 258 to 1693 under the artificial fragmentation schedule; the same exact chunks were produced. This is not a claim about performance of arbitrary network streams.
- BLAKE3 timings vary substantially across repeats. The observed differences cannot be attributed to stream granularity: JIT/tiered compilation, shared-host scheduling, CPU clock and cache state were not isolated. Three observations per lane are not enough to infer an improvement.
- `FileStream` cache residency is uncontrolled; its result is neither a cold-storage baseline nor a measurement of SSD physical throughput.
- Source `ReadBytes` are **not** internal `BytesCopied`. Whole-process CPU and allocation deltas can include activity outside the scanner; `WorkingSet64` is not peak RSS.
- 16 MiB/201 chunks is too small to characterize large manifests, LOH churn, concurrency or out-of-memory behavior; this run did not perform CSM manifest creation at all.
