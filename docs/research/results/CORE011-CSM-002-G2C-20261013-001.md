## Observed descriptive within-runner CPU/wall and allocations

Each original sample is **3 complete public calls per round**, **2 rounds** after warmup in an independent consumer OS process. Below, wall is milliseconds per call from the **larger of the two observed round values** (this is the current `guard.json` selection logic for an even number of samples, *not* a mathematical median). Repeat baseline was collected after both variants. Values are descriptive and noisy.

The stage256 increase for manifest-only Verify is **~9 KiB or more per call** versus baseline; this is consistent with one private 9,216-byte staging buffer plus object overhead and measurement noise. Stage64 is smaller but not allocation-neutral. No Gen2 collections or GC pauses were observed in these short rounds; this does not constitute independent retained-RSS or GC-pause noninferiority. RSS and private bytes from one short process are not a stable concurrency/idle memory result.

## Explicit verdict and forward gate

- **Correctness pilot:** **PASS**. All 24 public operation runs exactly matched their pinned CSM bytes and identities; source hash pins and worktree variants verified; targeted net8/net10 regression passed.
