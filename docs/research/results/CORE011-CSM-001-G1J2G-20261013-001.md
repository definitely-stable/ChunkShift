## Interpretation and subsequent controls

1. The physical CSP baseline proves that `MissingBytes=6697264` cannot be interpreted as 6.7 MiB of network patch payload. Existing compression/dictionary candidates already obtain a **1.26-MB base-aware physical patch** on this one pair.
2. J2F lab FastCDC16k's 149,568 reused target bytes may or may not further reduce **actual compressed CSP size**, because changing chunk boundaries also alters compression units, dictionary candidate selection and CSM/TCSM metadata. `149568 - 8460` is **not** a proved physical patch saving, nor a valid global compression bound.
3. The reported 840,848-B saved physical bytes are relative to the **self-contained current stable64k** CSP, *not* relative to another binary-diff product or a heldout corpus.
4. **Next:** predeclare a genuinely unseen managed binary version pair/product family and independent physical-patch comparison protocol. A lab profile needs honest experimental CSM identity and a complete exact reconstruction oracle, not forged registered public `ProfileId` values. Also separately attribute dictionary search cost; the observed create-wall difference is not suitable for ADOPT/REJECT.
