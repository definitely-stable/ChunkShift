## Engineering decision and research consequences

**Scoped negative product signal:** Neither tested managed binary release pair yields a smaller CSP from lab16k versus stable64k, despite genuine exact reuse. This contradicts the simple heuristic `more exact reuse -> better network patch` in these cases. Do not promote FastCDC16k to shipped default, use it as a public profile alias, or claim it beats stable64k on the general managed ecosystem.

Keep distinct issues:
- #154 CSM reader CPU/LOH/pooling production gate remains separate (real multi-CBLK mixed-reuse corpus and strict numerical decision budgets still absent); this J2I result does not resolve that reader gate.
- #221 PATCH-DOTNET-aware transforms remain research-only; no proof a CLR transform would beat the existing CSP compression/dictionary baseline.
- Next permitted research is **attribution of physical CSP payload/record overhead and dictionary-candidate time** on already frozen pairs using pre-registered instrumentation (no new profile variants), and then independent, prospectively selected families if proposing any production feature.
