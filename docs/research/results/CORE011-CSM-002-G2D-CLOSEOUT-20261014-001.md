### Quantitative findings and conflicts

- **G2-B hashing-only at 4,096 chunks:** BLAKE3 baseline ~322.17 µs versus stage256 ~256.80 µs (**−20.3%**); SHA256 baseline ~216.60 µs versus stage256 ~88.15 µs (**−59.3%**). These numbers apply to the isolated ManifestId kernel, **not** to complete CSM verification. G2-A's decode, hash and CRC kernels overlap and **must not be summed** into a purported total.
- **G2-C0 short paths (11/102 entries):** stage256 allocates approximately an extra **9 KiB per operation**, and end-to-end wall differences fall inside baseline order/drift. Unconditional per-manifest staging is not justified for these workloads.
- **G2-C1 multi-CBLK (5052 entries):** initial/repeated BLAKE3 Verify baseline **8.070/7.633 ms**, stage64 **7.311 ms**, stage256 **7.387 ms**; SHA256 Verify baseline **1.508/1.506 ms**, stage256 **1.163 ms**. Positive *within-runner descriptive signal*, not an adoption result: original and repeat baseline diverge, and there is just one runner, synthetic corpus and three measured rounds.
- **G2-C2 gated staging on independent hosted allocation:** BLAKE3 Verify baseline **24.067/24.113 ms**, gated stage256 **24.260 ms**; BLAKE3 Reader first/repeat baseline **24.600/7.617 ms**. Severe within-runner execution-phase variance invalidates extrapolation of G2-C1 gains to the new baseline. The 256-entry lazy gate **eliminates the extra stage array for short manifests** but preserves approximately +2 KiB(stage64)/+9 KiB(stage256) extra allocations on large calls.
- **G2-C3/G2-C4** reproduce strong runtime-mode and hardware effects under identical compiled production Core. EventPipe confirms real `QuickJitted`/`Optimized`/`OptimizedTier1` method publication, sometimes for BLAKE3 and ManifestId, but not the precise prior call-22 acceleration on new hardware. **JIT-tier presence does not prove causal responsibility** or candidate CPU benefit.

### Why this is a STOP for this research wave, not an algorithmic REJECT

1. There is **no frozen, reviewed product CPU/latency/allocation/LOH/retained-RSS budget**; G1-J decision guard remains `decisionRunsAllowed=false`, `budgetsLocked=false`. Therefore the current sample selection and wins cannot legally lead to ADOPT, however attractive a microbenchmark is.
2. Short manifests still pay reader overhead of order **~169–170 KiB allocated/call** (chiefly eager reader CBLK bytes), far greater than the ~2/9-KiB candidate stage buffer. Fixing the stage buffer alone does not resolve the larger allocation problem. It would be incorrect to assume that pooling the CBLK array improves RSS: G1-G/H/I already found retained pool buckets and cap/RSS complications.
3. Large multi-CBLK tests here use one deterministic 384-MiB synthetic source; they **do not qualify a sealed representative versioned mixed-reuse holdout**. Writer-side high-record-count Create path, short-read FileStream/fragmented input, 1/4/16 concurrent manifests, idle retention, GC Gen2/p95 and cancellation/error ordering have not jointly passed a frozen product decision.
4. CPU confounds remain materially larger than the marginal whole-operation gain for BLAKE3 on different runner allocations. G2-C3/C4 prove JIT-mode dependence, not the original CPU regime's cause. Selecting stage256 after inspecting G2-B/C1/C2 means those results cannot also be treated as an untouched candidate-selection holdout.
5. Original historic release closeout is already **DEFER**. This new scientific record updates *knowledge*, **not** the 0.1.1 release decision; revising the frozen old closeout would misrepresent what was known then.

**Disposition:** complete/stop G2 batching *research wave* as `CORRECTNESS_PASS / PRODUCT_NOT_QUALIFIED / DEFER / NO_ADOPT`. Keep production per-entry `ManifestIdAccumulator`, byte format, corruption ordering, hash suites, public SDK and default unchanged. Do not create G2-C5 or re-run another 384-MiB stage-width bakeoff without independently satisfying the reopen gate below.

## Conditional reopen gate for a separately authorised future product study

A *new issue/PR with new experiment ID* may propose production qualification only after ALL prerequisites are true **before viewing a decision run**:

1. **Owner and scope:** one explicit product question and a candidate frozen without tuning on the decision corpus (baseline, one width/gate only), identical source SHA and exact canonical CSM bytes. Do not retrospectively relabel old screen as a holdout.
2. **Representative physical source:** at least one real versioned, sealed mixed-reuse **multi-CBLK** corpus, plus predeclared 0/1/11/102/256/257/4096/5052/8193-record short/large boundaries as feasible; both BLAKE3 and SHA256, BIDX on/off. Frozen corpus origin, licensing, hashes and storage; no raw content in report.
3. **Product costs:** preregister full public **CreateAsync**, **VerifyManifestAsync**, complete **ManifestReader**, file-backed and fragmented/short-read variants; enforce exact CSM physical SHA, ManifestId/FileDigest, CRC and failed-CBLK early-entry exposure. Include cancellation, late-corruption, overflow and disposal.
4. **Numerical release budgets:** separately calibrate and review p50/p95 wall and process CPU tolerance, absolute small-manifest bytes/call, LOH/Gen2, RSS and **post-idle retention** at concurrency 1/4/16. Units, statistical method, minimum samples, disqualifiers, decision order and strict negative controls must be locked **before** final comparative execution. **No numerical budget can be manufactured from these G2 screens.**
5. **Runtime/environment:** separately pinned cold and steady-state warmup and tiered/PGO settings; at least two independently allocated GitHub-hosted runners on identical CPU class if possible, or hardware-stratified analysis (no pooled absolute timing between unequal CPUs). Verify target binary and suite hashes.
6. **Statistical decision:** independent, once-only sealed holdout; compare within-run paired baseline/candidate with baseline repeat, predecided outlier policy (never delete failed runs), uncertainty and explicit STOP/ADOPT/REJECT/DEFER. Any failed semantic or resource gate forces NO_ADOPT.

**Until these prerequisites are independently completed, the correct action is no further ManifestId batching iteration.**
