**Direct result:** process allocation **per call** is almost unchanged
across the 11-chunk and 5,052-chunk physical manifests, although
the physical metadata size grows from 830 B to 182,366 B.
Even a small short-manifest reader allocates approximately 165.5 KiB
to verify, compared with 179.3 KiB for full enumeration.
G1-D's independently observed fixed 147,528-byte per-call LOH
`System.Byte[]` signature is compatible with a dominant eager
147,500-byte `CsmStreamReaderCore._blockBuffer`.
It plausibly accounts for ~87% of verify managed allocation and ~80%
of enumerate managed allocation, but this is a **size-based hypothesis**,
not a stack/owner or lifetime certificate.

Gen2 totals vary mostly with worker count/call volume rather than
manifest chunk count. For verify, the 1/4/16-worker Gen2 totals were
`3/14/55` for the small manifest and `3/14/54` for multi-CBLK;
for enumerate, `3/14/49` vs `3/14/53`.
These are process-level observations, not proof that a particular
byte array alone caused a collection.

Batch processing the large manifest is slower in this pilot, as
expected when validating ~5,000 chunk records rather than 11.
But **batch wall is not single-call latency**; at 16 workers maxActive
was only 4–5 and the hosted machine had four logical CPUs.
No stable p95, CPU affinity, independent allocation stack or GC
causality inference is supplied. Residual RSS includes the previously
constructed fixture; post-GC LOH size is stale-last-GC footprint
rather than LOH allocation bytes or object ownership. Do not use
these as product peak memory.

## Engineering consequence / next gate

Prioritize a **three-way** lab-only design under #154 E3 rather
than blindly adopting `ArrayPool<byte>`:

1. Existing **eager maximum CBLK buffer**, fixed 147,500 B/reader.
2. **Lazy exact-sized CBLK buffer** allocated only after validating
   payload bounds, reused within one reader for subsequent CBLKs.
   This could avoid LOH altogether for short manifests without
   leaving LOH arrays in a process-wide pool.
3. **Bounded pooled buffer** retaining existing full-block
   pre-exposure CRC/semantic validation and exact CSM bytes.
   Measure actual bucket size and retained pool memory: requesting
   147,500 bytes from shared `ArrayPool<byte>` may return a
   262,144-byte bucket (~1.78x requested capacity).

Before any production promotion, (a) prove call-stack/source
allocation attribution, (b) isolate real CSM reader share in
end-to-end `PlanAsync`, patch create/apply and metadata-only
verify, (c) run strict matched untraced candidate A/B with
repeat-run stability, GC/LOH, RSS and corruption/fail-closed
oracle. A reduction in LOH *counts* alone is not a performance win.

The experiment remains `CORE011-CSM-001 = RUNNING`.
No API, default, NuGet, CSM v1 bytes, ManifestId or FileDigest
has been changed; **no ADOPT, REJECT or DEFER decision**.
