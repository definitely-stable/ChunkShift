## Interpretation and critical caveats

1. There were **zero Gen2 collections inside timed rounds**, even though large heap/LOH state was present after the deliberate initial GC (roughly 8–11 MiB depending on scenario). This does **not** establish that CSM buffers avoid the LOH or that pooling has no value. The 8-MiB input payload dominates the large-object footprint; this pilot cannot isolate CBLK LOH churn.
2. The recorded `lohLastGcSizeAfterBytes` is `GC.GetGCMemoryInfo().GenerationInfo[3].SizeAfterBytes`, i.e. the size observed *after the last completed collection*, **not LOH bytes allocated during the round**. The value can be stale in all 24 rounds.
3. `peakProcessWorkingSetBytes` is the lifetime peak of the scenario process, including fixture generation. It is **not per-round peak RSS**. `processAllocatedBytes` and CPU deltas are process-wide, and Task.Run scheduling is part of wall time.
4. SHA-256 always ran with BIDX disabled, while BLAKE3 ran with BIDX enabled. These two variables are confounded: the table must not be used to declare BLAKE3 slower or replace the hash backend. Such decisions belong to #127 and a separate end-to-end A/B lock.
5. The manifest is only ~4 KiB with 102 chunks; the pilot **does not cross a 4096-entry CBLK** and says nothing decisive about multi-block decoding, millions of entries, repeated LOH allocations or 16/64-way concurrency.
6. The four-worker gate recorded a max-active count of 3 or 4 depending on scenario. This confirms concurrent work entered the harness, not continuous physical overlap or linear scaling. Two rounds on a shared hosted runner cannot justify production speedup claims.

## Follow-on gate — what to implement next

- Extend the current CSM G1 experimental infrastructure to a **separately budgeted 384-MiB multi-CBLK** scenario on an adequately resourced GitHub-hosted runner, then repeated 1/4/16 worker *metadata-only* cases with matched hash/BIDX settings.
- Add event-based LOH allocation diagnostics or correctly isolated pool-retention measurements, not mislabeled `GenerationInfo[3]` counters; collect Gen2, allocation and RSS under thousands of repeated operations and retain all raw samples.
- Join those samples with matched-source `AmdahlHarness`, end-to-end CSM baseline, and real FileStream/short-read source lanes before prioritizing `CORE011-CSM-002` (ManifestId batching), -003 (decode once), -004 (pooling) or -006 (CRC Amdahl).
- Keep `CORE011-CSM-001` **RUNNING**, no ADOPT/REJECT/DEFER. Frozen Core 0.1.0 API, stable profile/digests, CSM v1 persisted bytes and package contract remain untouched.
