**Observed structural signal:** at one worker, none of the 8 source/BIDX/operation scenarios produced a Gen2 collection during timed rounds; at 4 concurrent BLAKE3/BIDX-on workers, each operation family recorded 1 Gen2, and at 16 workers each recorded 5 Gen2. That is a reproducible measurement *within this run*, not evidence of causal CBLK-buffer churn or proof that pooling helps. Input is 384 MiB but the real CSM is ~182 KiB; process allocation scales with total call count and worker workload. The forced GC *after* rounds is outside timing.

## Attribution limits — mandatory for any later decision

1. The 384-MiB fixture was released and a `WeakReference` check proved its managed array collectible before timing. Nevertheless, **lifetime `PeakWorkingSet64` and current process RSS may still reflect memory that was resident during fixture construction**; they are not per-round CSM-reader peak RSS. The post-GC managed heap is ~0.54–0.59 MiB depending on lane. Do not claim that the residual ~460 MiB physical WorkingSet belongs to CSM metadata.
2. `GC.GetGCMemoryInfo().GenerationInfo[3].SizeAfterBytes` is size *after the last completed GC*, not LOH allocation bytes in a timed round; absent GC it is stale. The explicit final GC produces a separate retention snapshot, not per-operation LOH allocation attribution. Post-GC LOH size alone does not identify which arrays were retained.
3. Worker-batch sizes are **4, 16 and 64 operations**, respectively, so a 16-worker batch taking longer than a single-worker batch is not necessarily a regression. Worker `maxActive` is diagnostic, not a CPU affinity or parallel-speedup guarantee. Two rounds on shared hosted CI are not sufficient for stable p95 or a performance verdict.
4. The suite/BIDX controls are balanced for **one worker**. The 4/16-worker lanes only use BLAKE3/BIDX on. No 4/16-worker BIDX or hash comparison, no FileStream/short-read source, no 1M/10M/100M-entry manifest, and no native memory or event-based LOH allocation trace is included.
5. CSM content verification and creation were done outside timed metadata-only operations. Do not subtract these reader measurements from unrelated scanner/Amdahl measurements as if perfectly matched stages.

## Next scientifically justified step

Prioritize a **direct, isolated LOH-allocation and buffer-retention trace** at the multi-CBLK boundary (e.g. runtime EventPipe/GC allocation events with trace completeness and overhead accounted for), plus a preregistered sustained 1/4/16-worker repeat-run matrix on the same physical CSM and fixed hash/BIDX. Attribute CSM reader and CBLK array lifetimes before implementing pooled CBLK buffers. Only if this overhead is material in *matched end-to-end* create/read/verify workloads should #154 E3 pooling or E2 decode-once be prototyped. E1 ManifestId batching and E5 CRC must pass their own Amdahl gate.
